# OG Image Brief — Silly Travel Tool
**Output:** 1200×630 PNG → save as `public/og-image.png`

## Visual Direction
A clean, magazine-editorial travel poster with the Silly Travel Tool brand aesthetic.

**Background:** Warm terracotta (#C4561E) with a subtle diagonal grid pattern (like graph paper, 10% opacity white lines)

**Left half (60%):**
- The Goose mascot, large and confident — a stylised white goose in a slightly editorial illustration style
- Goose should look like it *knows* something — slightly smug, head tilted, one wing raised as if pointing "that way"
- Optional: small suitcase or passport near its feet
- Goose rendered in clean white/off-white with black outlines — flat illustration style, not 3D/realistic

**Right half (40%), vertically centred:**
- Large wordmark: **"Silly Travel Tool"** in a bold, slightly quirky sans-serif (like Space Grotesk)
- Colour: white
- Below it, smaller: **"Trust The Goose."** in warm off-white (#FAF7F2), italic or lighter weight
- Small goose emoji 🪿 before the tagline

**Overall feel:** Confident, warm, slightly playful — like a boutique travel magazine cover, not a generic app screenshot.

## Do Not
- No drop shadows on text
- No gradient backgrounds
- No stock travel photos
- No cluttered UI elements

## Colour Palette
- Primary: #C4561E (terracotta)
- Text: #FFFFFF, #FAF7F2
- Grid lines: rgba(255,255,255,0.08)
- Goose: #F0EBE3 body, #2A2520 outlines, #E8A820 beak

## Tools to use
Gemini Imagen, Midjourney, or Adobe Firefly — whichever gives the cleanest flat illustration result.
Prompt hint for Midjourney: `editorial travel poster, confident cartoon white goose mascot, terracotta background, bold typography "Silly Travel Tool", warm minimal aesthetic, flat illustration, clean lines --ar 1200:630 --style raw`
