FORWARD IT, DONE
Forward a flight, hotel, train, restaurant or ticket confirmation to your private Goose inbox. Goose reads it and files it into the right trip, with every time shown in the local time where it happens. No email? Paste the confirmation text, or add a booking by hand.

YOUR TRIP, DAY BY DAY
Flights, stays, trains and plans laid out day by day, with the weather for every stop. Visiting several cities? Keep one trip and add a stay in each. While a trip is on, Today shows what's next, how long until it, and directions to get there.

THE GUIDE
City guides for Know, Eat, See, Transit and Events. Facts like addresses, ratings and hours come from Google; commentary written by Goose (AI) is labelled and can be reported.

IN YOUR POCKET
