How to use Silly Travel Tool

Short guides to Silly Travel Tool · Updated 2026-10-29

The short version. Start a trip, forward your booking emails to your Goose inbox, and Goose files them into the right trip with local times. Invite people to a shared trip, check the weather for your dates, explore the city guide or ask HONK for nearby picks, and keep currency, phrases and SOS numbers in your pocket.

Getting started

Start a trip

A trip is a city and, when you know them, the dates.

  1. Open Trips and tap New trip.
  2. Pick the city. Add dates, or tick "Not sure of the dates yet".
  3. Save. The trip opens on its Plan tab, ready for bookings.
Good to know

A trip without dates shows "Planning". Tap Add dates whenever you decide; adding a booking fills them in for you.

Visiting several cities? Keep one trip and add a stay in each city. Weather follows you city by city.

Forward booking emails

A quick way in. Goose reads the email and files it into the right trip.

  1. Find your private Goose inbox address in Profile → Settings → Goose inbox, and tap Copy.
  2. Forward any flight, hotel, train, restaurant or ticket confirmation to it.
  3. It shows up in the matching trip. No trip for that city yet? Goose starts one.
Good to know

Save the address as a contact called "Goose" so forwarding takes two taps.

Keep the address private: anything sent to it lands in your trips.

Paste a confirmation

For bookings that live in an app, a PDF or a screenshot instead of your inbox.

  1. Open a trip, tap + and choose Paste confirmation text.
  2. Paste the text and tap Read it. Goose pulls out times, places and confirmation numbers.
  3. Check the details, pick the trip (or New trip) and save.

Add or edit a booking by hand

Flights, stays, trains, restaurants, tickets, cars, buses and ferries, or a plain note.

  1. Open a trip, tap + and pick a type under "Or add by hand".
  2. Fill in what you have. Restaurants and tickets only need the place, date and time; the rest sits under More details.
  3. Tap any booking later to see it, edit it or delete it.
Good to know

Times are always the local time where the thing happens, not your phone's time. A 09:00 flight from Tokyo stays 09:00, wherever you are.

Travel together

Invite people to a trip

Invite by email so everyone plans from the same trip.

  1. Open the trip and tap the Travelers button at the top (or Trip options → Invite travelers).
  2. Type their email under Invite by email, choose Can edit or View only, and tap Send invite.
  3. Goose emails them an invite. They sign in (or create a free account) and tap Join trip under Invites on their Trips screen.
Good to know

Someone who accepts can see the whole shared trip, including its booking details, activities and dates. They never see your travel documents or loyalty cards.

View only stops edits, not viewing: it keeps the plan safe from accidental changes, but a View only traveller can still see the trip.

If the trip owner deletes their account, the trip is deleted for everyone.

See changes live

While a trip is open, changes from other travellers appear without a refresh.

  1. Keep the trip open: new bookings and plans appear on their own.
  2. Times are shown in the local time where they happen, so arrivals line up even across time zones.
  3. Add a plan like "Meet at the hotel, 16:30" so everyone knows where to be.

Who can do what

The trip owner decides who can edit and who can only view.

  1. Can edit: add, change and remove bookings and plans.
  2. View only: see everything, change nothing.
  3. The owner can change access, cancel an invite or remove someone from the Travelers sheet. Anyone else can tap Leave trip there.

Explore a city

City guide

Know, Eat, See, Transit and Events for any city.

  1. Open Explore. Your next trip's city loads on its own, or search any city.
  2. Pick a section: Know (history and local quirks), Eat (cheap to splurge), See, Transit or Events.
  3. Tap Show 15 more for the next batch of places.
Good to know

Facts like addresses, ratings and hours come from Google. The commentary is written by Goose (AI) and labelled; tap Report if something is off.

HONK: what's good near me

Pick a mood and get nearby place picks.

  1. Open Explore and tap a mood on the HONK card, or More moods.
  2. Your browser asks to share your location. HONK uses it only when you allow it. If you would rather not, choose a city yourself.
  3. Goose picks a place and shows its walking time. Not feeling it? Tap Another spot.
Good to know

Moods include I'm hungry, Treat myself, On a budget, Culture me and Night out.

Your location is sent to Google to find nearby places. See the Privacy Policy.

On the trip

Plan, Weather and Pack

Each trip has three tabs.

  1. Plan: everything booked, day by day, plus your trip ideas.
  2. Weather: the forecast for each day, for the city you're in that night.
  3. Pack: a packing list made for this trip, its weather and what you've booked. Tick things off as you go. The list is kept on this device.
Good to know

While a trip is on, Trips opens on Today: what's next, how long until it, and buttons to open the booking or get directions.

When a trip ends you get a Recap you can share.

Travel tools

Currency, Translate, Tip & split and SOS, set for the country of your current or next trip.

  1. Currency: live rates between the local currency and your home currency.
  2. Translate: a pocket phrase book in the local language, plus a box to translate anything you type.
  3. Tip & split: each person's share of the bill.
  4. SOS: emergency numbers and an emergency card for the country you're in.
Good to know

Numbers can change or be wrong; in an emergency follow local authorities.

Phrase books you've loaded and typed translations are kept on your device.

Your account

Travel documents and loyalty cards

Passports, visas, insurance and frequent-flyer numbers in one place.

  1. Open Profile and tap Travel documents or Loyalty cards.
  2. Add a document or card. Document numbers are encrypted and show only the last four digits until you tap Reveal.
Good to know

Your documents and loyalty cards are private to you. They are not shown to people you share a trip with.

Goose score and badges

Points for your trips, countries, days abroad, flights and badges.

  1. Your score and stats are at the top of Profile.
  2. Tap Badges to see what you've earned and how close you are to the next one.
Good to know

Your score and badges are personal: they are private to you.

Settings and privacy

Your name, home currency, light or dark mode, °C or °F.

  1. Open Profile → Settings.
  2. Turn off Share usage stats to stop anonymous feature counts from this device.
  3. Delete account erases your trips and documents (payment records are kept as the law requires). Bookings you added to someone else's trip stay on that trip.

Questions

Do the people I invite need STT?

Yes, a free account. Goose emails them an invite; once they sign in with that email they tap Join trip under Invites on their Trips screen.

Something isn't working. How do I tell you?

Every report helps. Tap Report an issue at the bottom of Profile or Help: it opens an email with your app version filled in. Say what happened and what you expected, and add a screenshot if you can.

I forwarded an email but nothing showed up.

Give it a minute, then close and reopen the app. Check you sent it to your own Goose inbox address (Profile → Settings). Still nothing? Open the trip, tap + and paste the confirmation text instead.

The times look wrong.

Every time in STT is the local time where it happens, so a flight shows its departure city's time and its arrival city's time. If a booking was entered in the wrong zone, open it and tap Edit booking.

Goose put a booking in the wrong trip.

Open the booking, delete it and paste the confirmation into the right trip. Or paste it from Trips and pick the trip yourself.

Does it work offline?

Phrase books you've loaded and typed translations are kept on your device. Guides, imports and live rates need a connection, so screenshot SOS and your hotel address before you fly.

Who can see my trips?

Your trips are private to you unless you invite someone. Goose's AI processors read forwarded emails to pull out booking details; we keep the booking details. See the Privacy Policy.
