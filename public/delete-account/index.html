Delete your account

Silly Travel Tool, operated by B&B Project Factory

In the app (fastest)

Open Silly Travel Tool on the web, iPhone or Android, sign in, then go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account is erased right away.

By email

If you can't sign in, email hello@sillytraveltool.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm it's you and delete it within 30 days.

What gets erased

  • Your account, profile, email address and private Goose inbox address
  • Trips you own, with their bookings, stops, ideas, packing lists and invites
  • Travel documents, loyalty cards, badges, scores and usage history
  • Bookings you added to someone else's shared trip stay with that trip's owner

What we keep, and for how long

  • Payment records (amount, date, refunds) without your account, as tax law requires (up to 7 years)
  • Pseudonymous cost and usage counts with nothing that identifies you
  • Backups roll over and are gone within 30 days

Subscriptions

A First Class subscription bought on our website is cancelled when you delete your account. A subscription bought in the App Store or Google Play is not: cancel it in your Apple or Google account so you aren't charged again.
