Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version. We keep your trips, bookings and travel documents so the app can show them to you. We also look at how the app is used, and at the kind of trips you plan, to improve STT and to tell you about things that fit your travel. We don't sell your data, don't show ads and don't share it with advertisers. Some features use AI and other services, listed below, which only get what they need to do their job. You can turn usage stats off, turn tailored offers off, and ask us to delete everything at any time.

Who we are

Silly Travel Tool ("STT", "we") is operated by B&B Project Factory, 402 Grandey Way NE, Renton, WA 98056, USA. Questions or requests: hello@sillytraveltool.com.

What we collect

Your account

Your email address, display name and (if you sign in with Google) your Google profile name and picture. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and we never see it.

Trips and bookings

Trips, bookings (flights, stays, trains, restaurants, tickets, cars, notes), packing lists and anything you type into them, including confirmation numbers and prices. If you share a trip, the people you invite can see it and we store their email address to send the invite.

Booking emails and files you import

Each account has a private Goose inbox address. Emails sent to it are received by our email provider, read by our AI to pull out booking details, and turned into bookings in your trips. Files you upload to import (PDFs, screenshots, photos, spreadsheets) are read the same way. We keep the extracted details, not a library of your emails or files. Goose only reads them once you've allowed it (see AI features); until then, forwarded emails wait unread and we keep just the sender's domain and the subject so the app can show them to you.

Beta waitlist

If you join the beta list at sillytraveltool.com/beta, we keep your email address, the platform you picked, how often you travel (if you say), the campaign tags in the link you came from and the site that sent you. We use them only to email you about the beta (a confirmation, then your invite) and to count where sign-ups come from. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To limit abuse, we keep a one-way hash of your IP address for a day. We send these emails with Resend.

Travel documents and loyalty cards

Documents you add (for example passport, visa, insurance, vaccination record) and loyalty cards. Passport and document numbers are encrypted at rest; the app shows only the last four digits until you tap Reveal.

Location

Only when you allow it, and only on your device's request: SOS uses it to show the right emergency numbers, and HONK uses it to find spots near you. Your coordinates are turned into a city and country by a geocoding service; only the city and country are sent to our AI. We don't store your location history.

Payments

If you buy First Class on the website, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your purchase details (plan, amount, date, status, refunds and any discount code) but never your full card number. In the apps, purchases go through Apple or Google, who share the purchase status with us through RevenueCat. Codes you redeem are recorded with your account.

Usage and diagnostics

Product events (for example "trip created", "guide viewed", which screen was opened and how long it was actively in use), plus once per session a few device settings: device type (phone, tablet or computer), operating system, whether you use the app, an installed web app or a browser, language, time zone, light or dark theme, connection type and how many days since your last visit. When you first arrive we note where you came from (the referring website or a campaign tag in the link) and save it with your account when you sign up. Events carry only short codes: no names, emails, trip titles, confirmation or document numbers, typed text or exact locations. They are stored with your account in our database and also sent to Google Analytics, on the website and in the apps, with a random device identifier and your account's random ID (never your email), IP addresses anonymised and no advertising features. If the app crashes we may receive an error report without personal data.

Your choice: turn off Settings → Share usage stats to stop usage events, session details and source tracking on that device. We do not send crash reports when you turn off usage statistics. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, it starts off.

Your traveller profile

From the trips and bookings you add, we work out a short profile used inside STT: for example how often and how long you travel, how far ahead you plan, whether you travel solo or with others, the regions you visit, how you usually book, and a broad travel-style band (from value to luxury) based on cabin class, hotel prices, room types and loyalty status you've saved. It's never shown publicly, never sold, never used to change prices or what you can use, and never used to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. We use it to understand our travellers as a group, to decide what to build, to choose partners and, with your consent where the law requires it, to send you offers that fit your trips.

Your choice: turn off Settings → Tailored offers and your profile is left out of offer targeting and audience reports.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: show your trips, import bookings, build guides, packing lists and phrasebooks.
  • To keep your account and the service secure and prevent abuse: rate limits, reviewing unusual activity, and restricting or suspending accounts that break our Terms.
  • To support you: when you contact us, our team can look at your account details, your trips' places, dates and counts, your plan and your recent app activity. Access is limited to our admins, needs a verified sign-in and is logged. Booking details such as confirmation numbers are not shown in these tools; we only open them to answer a data request from you.
  • To improve the app: usage events and traveller profiles, looked at per member and as a group.
  • To manage payments, codes and refunds.
  • To tell you about things that fit your travel. No marketing emails without your consent, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

Where the law asks for a legal basis: performing our contract with you (running the app, payments, support), our legitimate interests in keeping it secure, improving it and understanding our travellers (including usage stats and traveller profiles, which you can turn off), your consent for location and for marketing emails, and legal obligations (for example tax records for payments).

AI features

Goose uses AI to read what you send it: booking emails you forward, text and files you import, and sentences you type to translate. We ask first: you'll see "Let Goose use AI on your bookings?" in the tour or the first time you import, and you can change your answer any time in Settings → AI reading of imports. Forwarded emails and pasted text (the subject, the sender and the first part of the message) are read by DeepSeek, whose servers are in China. If DeepSeek isn't available, Google Gemini (United States) reads them instead. PDFs and images go to Google Gemini. They receive only what you send to Goose, not your account details. These companies process the data on their own servers, and each company's own rules apply to what they keep. We keep the booking details, not the emails. If you say no, email import, pasting a confirmation and importing an itinerary won't work; you can still add bookings by hand. Other AI features send less: city guides send only the city and country; packing lists send the destination, trip length, month and the kinds of bookings you have; HONK picks send the city, country and what you asked for; translating sends the sentence you type.

Text written by Goose is generated by AI and marked "Suggested by Goose (AI)". It can be wrong; check anything important before you rely on it, and use the Report link to tell us about bad suggestions.

Account decisions

To protect travellers and the service, we may warn, limit features on, suspend or close accounts that break our Terms, for example by automating AI features or abusing invites. A person reviews these decisions, the reason is recorded, and you can reply to the email we send to ask us to look again.

How long we keep it

Account, trip and booking data: until you delete it or your account. Usage events: 25 months, then deleted automatically. Payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually 7 years), even after account deletion. Records of moderation decisions (for example a ban for abuse): as long as needed to keep the service safe. When your account is deleted we erase everything else within 30 days, including backups. Beta waitlist entries: until the beta ends or you ask us to delete yours (email us).

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete trips, bookings, documents and cards in the app at any time.
  • Turn location off in your phone's settings; the app keeps working without it.
  • Turn off Settings → Share usage stats to stop usage events and session details on that device.
  • Turn off Settings → Tailored offers to keep your traveller profile out of offers and audience reports, or unsubscribe from any marketing email.
  • Manage purchases: website purchases through the link in your receipt; app purchases in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Turn off Settings → AI reading of imports to stop Goose's AI reading new emails, imports and translations; forwarded emails then wait for your OK.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account, type DELETE and confirm; your account is erased right away. If you can't sign in, email hello@sillytraveltool.com from your account address. See what is erased and what we keep.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or that we stop a particular use (including objecting to profiling), by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA) you have these rights by law, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS, access is limited per account by database rules, AI and payment keys stay on our servers, admin access is checked and logged, and document numbers are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong we'll tell affected users as the law requires.

International transfers

Our main servers are in the United States, and AI requests are processed by DeepSeek in China (with Google in the United States for photos, PDFs and as backup). If you use STT from elsewhere, your data is transferred to those countries. For our US providers we rely on their contractual safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses where they apply). China doesn't have an EU or UK adequacy decision and we don't have such safeguards with DeepSeek, so for what Goose's AI reads we rely on the permission you give in the app, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Children

STT is not meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK). We don't knowingly collect their data; if you believe a child has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
